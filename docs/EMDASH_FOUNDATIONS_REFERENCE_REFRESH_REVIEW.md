## Finding and correction

The book/article revision was complete, but the standing references had only
received a partial profile update. Their introductions mentioned the new
foundations while substantive sections still described retired strict-functor
wrappers, unshifted family opposites, the old Homd target, or deferred work
that had since been installed. The user explicitly requested correction of
those descriptions, rather than another banner directing readers elsewhere.

The current sections now describe the installed source directly:

- public strict functors and conventional lax transformations, with a strict
  pre/right leg and retained post/left comparison;
- `Hom_Cat = Functor_cat`, the distinct Cartesian coefficient and mapping
  category, and admitted families/ordinary Hom valued in `Cat_cart`;
- T, R, O and C₂, their whole coefficient-map types, and the shifted bases of
  `Op_catd` and `TotalOp_catd`;
- the actual identity/relative Homd construction, Q/P classifiers, whole
  y-transformation and direct x/u/y/v/f projection types;
- the negative-index Sigma–Hom and forward fibre arrows;
- Pi's terminal-source presentation and the explicit forward
  `sigma_functord_sec_func`, without the removed whole-category/carrier folds;
- total-dual negative sections for `Hom_catd`, Cartesian fibres of
  `Functor_catd`, and the corresponding mixed transformation profile;
- strict functor objects in Gray interfaces, retained lax transformations,
  actual pseudo inverse data where an API still consumes it, and retirement
  of the duplicate strict-functor carrier grammar;
- installed production behavior versus isolated native candidates, and the
  original frontend acceptance versus the later Pi/Sigma adaptation.

These are in-place revisions of the main descriptions and examples. Historical
diagnostics retain their source revisions; they are not presented as the
current type system.

# Strict-functor/duality migration: unresolved work and regression ledger

Date: 2026-10-20 (UTC)
Plan-ID: STRICT-FUNCTOR-DUALITY-FOUNDATIONS-V3.2
Status: active; inventory reviewed against `708bb2de` and its emitted source
Owner: [living foundations plan](EMDASH_STRICT_FUNCTOR_DUALITY_FOUNDATIONS_PLAN.md)

This is the consolidated inventory of known remaining obligations, changed
contracts, rejected alternatives and optional follow-up work. It complements
the chronological plan and the detailed owner reviews; it does not replace
their source pins or receipts. An old review saying “next” or “open” is not
automatically a current blocker. Later consumer evidence may supersede it.
Conversely, a passing subset does not discharge an omitted assertion.

The initial source migration can advance past an independent unresolved
observation. Production promotion and completion cannot advance past a broken
required computation. “Revisit after the basic migration” below means before
production qualification for the required rows, and after this goal only for
the explicitly optional rows. No row authorizes a new foundational redesign.

## Current boundary

The selected design still has strict public functors, conventional lax
transformations and displayed maps, public `Hom_Cat = Functor_cat`, and
`Cat_cart` coefficients for admitted families and representable Hom families.
The native identity Homd is followed by an actual composite for general FF.
Q's outer mapping category is Cartesian; its inner `Homd_index_cat` retains
the directed comparisons required by intrinsic action. No new path/equality
argument is supplied by callers.

All implementation remains in the non-library replay. The production kernel
has not been promoted. The [public-ladder evidence][public] qualifies fourteen
general/relative observations; the five terminal-source observations remain
positive obligations in the full diagnostic. The qualified central subset
contains 1327 positive and 205 negative assertions; the full source retains
2146 positives, 293 negatives and all 66 original diagnostic declarations.
These counts describe different scopes and must not be interchanged.

## Terminal alternatives: what was and was not investigated

The previous rejection was of specific implementations, not an impossibility
result. The search is not exhaustive. The five assertions also concern three
different operations (R01–R03); one broad terminal rule need not be their best
shared implementation.

| Approach | Evidence/status | Assessment and next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normalize terminal covariance to a chosen unit input | Failed the neutral-unit projection. | Reject that rule. Covariance still sees its supplied object before Hom erases endpoints. |
| Canonical terminal-map/covariance normalization | Failed subject reduction at the higher projection, which would equate unit variables with `Terminal_obj`. | Reject that combined normalizer. This does not rule out canonical strict terminal maps as a general construction. |
| Normalize every terminal conjoint and terminal mate | Some whole ladder and core tests passed; the independent right-unit cut failed after the conjoint representation disappeared. Two relative assertions had also been replaced by their composites in that experiment. | Not adopted. Neither the passing tests nor the replacement assertions establish the five original obligations. |
| Normalize only a conjoint whose underlying transport is explicitly constant | Passes original whole assertion 13, both native Q units and a typed nonterminal control. Fails the original conjoint-composition law; two added warning bodies identify the two composition orientations. [Exact evidence][alternatives]. | Reject this eager rule as a standalone repair. It preserves more than the unrestricted candidate, but still erases a representation used by composition. It does not solve R01 or R03. |
| Preserve represented heads; simplify their existing ordinary projections | Existing architecture already separates conjoint/represented owners from their semantic bodies. No complete terminal solution qualified. | Preferred direction when an eager whole rewrite would lose unit or representation cuts. For a whole terminal equality, identify the existing comparison owner; do not substitute point checks for it. |
| Repair normalized units at the Q unit/composition owner | Not implemented as a complete alternative. | Legitimate only if the normalized unit is the selected normal form. Must also preserve represented-inclusion projections and both composition orders. Adding just a special right-unit clause would be insufficient evidence. |
| Put terminal computation at the native identity-Homd owner | Not qualified. Current `homd_id_src_func`/`homd_id_tgt_func` are defined composites, so rules matching those transparent names would be inert/brittle. | Inspect their actual constituent owners first. Do not create a primitive merely to name a failing expression. A change of whole ownership needs a concrete consumer and complete projections. |
| Keep a stable runtime form and provide a whole semantic comparison | Possible fallback, not a demonstrated solution of the old runtime assertions. | A proof-time view must be explicitly distinguished from runtime conversion, with the affected contract reviewed. It cannot be counted as the original reduction without justification. |
| General terminal-source eta or global strengthening of FF | No such change selected. | Too broad for this repair. It would affect unrelated functors, neutral unit terms and retained lax comparisons. The current evidence does not justify it. |

No operational proof argument, enlarged `(y,v,f)` base or general coend
development is a proposed repair here. A narrower successful alternative
should replace only the affected owner slice; the other obligations remain.

The new represented-inclusion observation is also instructive: observing the
whole Hom yields raw composition while the capped inclusion yields the stable
`Homd_coeff_rep` head. Runtime equality fails in the unchanged source too;
the existing whole proof-time comparison passes. This is an intended
presentation distinction to retain in R06, not a newly discovered invalid
typing or a regression caused by the terminal experiment.

## Deliberate interface changes to audit for compatibility

These are migration contracts, not automatically defects. Their full consumer
audit is R13. The chronological plan retains the detailed reasons and receipts.

| Surface | Selected contract and preservation check |
| --- | --- |
| `Functor` / `fapp*` | Strict by default, with the same public names. Old lax-functor nonconversion assertions change only when this contract explains the change. Preserve genuinely lax transfor cells. |
| `Transf` / `Functord` | Conventional lax, pre/right strict and post/left retained; no blanket strictness for Cat-valued transfors. General FF remains lax. Identity-core/defined-relative Homd separates their computation. |
| Families and Hom families | Both use `K → Cat_cart`; the public universe still has `Hom_Cat = Functor_cat`. Fixed-base displayed maps remain lax; some varying-base operations require Cartesian diagram categories. |
| Dualities | `Op_cat` is dimension-one transpose; `CoAbove2_cat` reverses dimensions ≥2; `TotalOp_cat` is their composite. `CoAt2_cat` and shifted bases occur where the action requires them. A total-duality alias is not an LHS constructor. |
| Displayed opposite | `Op_funcd` has the Cartesian-map profile and a shifted base. Raw lax FF is not silently admitted; the intrinsic-action route retains it without that operation. |
| Evaluation, curry, exchange | Joint strict Cartesian operations use their actual Cartesian classifiers. Fixed evaluation/whiskering and the existing one-way lax curry retain their broader action. No unrestricted Cartesian lax uncurry is promised. |
| Π and its evaluation | Π remains the ordinary lax transformation-backed section category. `pi_eval_transf` is section-first; the old k-first displayed interface is not restored by equating its incompatible classifiers. |
| Q and Homd | Q's curried Hom is a coefficient view; the existing product-based Prof convention is retained. The v-result has an ordinary presheaf view, not an inclusion of its entire lax index category into Cartesian presheaves. Preserve actual represented maps, units and higher data. |
| Sigma/graphs/slices | Correct total dualities and family coefficients are part of the construction. The existing slice triangle direction differs from CommaFib and is retained. Check actual generic Sigma maps, not the earlier raw-Cat counterexample under different coefficients. |
| Canonical Hom annotations | Capped action uses `Obj(Hom_cat ...)` for its result. Its original input Hom is retained after canonicalizing both lost opposite-domain inference. No endpoint is reconstructed by an invalid injectivity assumption. |
| Inverses and assembly | Chosen inverse data, OneCat/discrete guards and supplied assembly/model hypotheses remain actual inputs. An ordinary inverse does not automatically admit a Cartesian inverse. Migrating a supplied assembly principle does not derive that principle. |

## Maintenance rule

At each bounded checkpoint, update the affected row with its exact owner,
consumer, disposition and evidence. New failures must be classified as a
required behavior, deliberate interface change, inherited limitation, or
optional improvement. “Passing elsewhere,” extra resource headroom and a
pointwise replacement are not dispositions for a whole failed obligation.

The inventory covers known issues in the active plan, current replay and
`production_*_review.md` records at the stated checkpoint. It is not a claim
that all possible alternatives or future regressions have been discovered.

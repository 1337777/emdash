# Lift action: bounded source closure and qualification

The paired-unit variant checks the structural represented lift's whole and
capped right action, both identity-arrow projection orders, the existing
fixed-source internal-action projection, and an actual nonidentity represented
x-action instance. Lift-identity components also check. The actual mate at
an identity arrow and represented companion composition remain failed positive
obligations. No triangle, whole lift-identity/composition, or independent
laxity law is installed.

The [recipe](lift_action_migration.py) emits 25 non-library LP targets without
invoking Lambdapi. [Input pins](lift_action_inputs.json) retain the exact donor,
predecessor verification file and package file. The
[verification record](lift_action_verification.json) records emitted and
authoring hashes, fifteen current checked-target receipts, one archived historical
failure, warning comparison, and static LHS audit. Other emitted targets are
unqualified until separately checked; source assertion counts are inventory,
not evidence that failed targets completed their remaining assertions.

Two [action projections](lift_action.lpfragment) are inserted after the whole
mate and before the Homd y-section owner. They select the existing actual

```text

R_U(x,y)[q] = post_Q(kappa(U[y]))[(iota D)[q]].
```

The capped rule completes the other projection order of that same whole
action. Its input is the existing strict diagram transformation U. Raw FF
retains its separate covariant `Prof_cov_diagram_map` and is rejected as a
strict represented-lift input.

The [right coefficient-unit join](lift_action_coefficient_units.lpfragment)
and its [measured left mirror](lift_action_coefficient_left_unit.lpfragment)
are inserted at the existing `q_unit_hom_units` position, immediately before
the derived represented-unit view. Each recognizes only the actual Unit-curry
representation of a Q identity after its earlier projection:

```text
post_Q(P)[Rep_A] = P : Q(A,B)
pre_Q(P)[Rep_B]  = P : Q(A,B).
```

The actual repeated unit endpoint remains a pattern guard. These are
projection-order completions of coefficient unit computation. Neither rule
folds general post/precomposition to raw composition, special-cases only
capped lift action, or asserts a unit/counit triangle.

Every insertion anchor is unique and checked. Earlier action and right-only
targets retain their exact bytes; the paired-unit targets are separate
variants. No predecessor builder or production source is changed by this
recipe. Composition with the identity-core migration belongs to its separate
assembly recipe and does not inherit qualification automatically.

## Remaining failures after endpoint qualification

The passing paired-unit controls establish

```text
L_U(x,x)[id_x] = kappa(U[x]) = R_U(x,x)[id_x].
```

The remaining mate-identity assertion compares the actual composite of the
canonical unit/counit step components with `id(kappa(U[x]))`. Its failure is
therefore no longer caused by either of those two endpoint normal forms.
It is the unresolved canonical triangle computation. The later native
diagnostic prints both sides: the mate's outer composite source and target
are byte-identical to each other and to the object of the comparison identity.
The remaining issue lies in the cell factors, not those outer endpoints.
This does not exclude further justified projection joins at the canonical
unit/counit owners, and is not a mathematical counterexample or nonexistence
proof.

A subsequent read-only inventory identifies existing omitted computation
owners. [q_unit_migration.py](q_unit_migration.py) moves the shaped classifiers,
`Prof_id_hom`, `Prof_func_hom` with its action, and the declaration of
`Prof_tensor_hom_hom` into the early copy. It does not move `Prof_cell_apply`,
the left/right co-Yoneda maps or transformations, or their tensor-introduction
beta/fusion rules from [the later production owners](../../emdash3_2.lp).

rules therefore does not show that the actual mate reaches their redexes.

The intermediate object retains the rigid `Hom_fapp0` owner. Exact term,

application bridge remains open; no triangle law follows from this trace.

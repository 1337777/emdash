This non-library experiment now constructs the whole represented mate using
the original unit/counit step bodies and an explicit common middle M. Both
required whole-middle comparisons, the final composite, and its first actual
component consumer check. Removing any of the three selected rule groups
breaks the final mate check. Full lift
action/coherence and local raw-prof admission remain open; no full Q
qualification or production computation is promoted. Earlier failed positive
obligations retain their exact source and receipts.

without invoking Lambdapi. The [verification record](lift_middle_verification.json)
pins 44 generated LP targets, 40 existing receipts, their logs and output
summaries, authoring inputs, package input, checker identity, and resource
settings. Consolidation reproduced every existing target byte-for-byte.

pinned source and receipt evidence; their authoring recipes are not yet part
of this builder. One superseded syntax-only failure is retained with its
content-addressed source archive. Evidence validation checks 44 receipts in
total, keeping that historical failure separate from current-target outcomes.

## Mathematical scope

For explicitly strict `U : E => D`, the common whole functor is

```text
M = Hom_Q(kappa(U[x]), kappa(U[y])) o iota_1 o U_1.
```

The unit-step target and counit-step source must each agree with M. The
candidate generic laws are projection-order and association completions of
existing lawful strict-functor laws and guarded strict-transfor laws. The
three whiskering unifiers express whole Hom factorizations at proof time;
typed `eq_refl` controls exercise them. Actual arrows remain constrained,
with perturbed-arrow negative controls. Component agreement alone does not
supply whole agreement, and no operational comparison argument is
added to the lift.

The current design preference for primitive identity Homd and a defined
FF-relative alias changes the proposed interface and dependency routing. It
does not remove the whole represented-lift dependency. U's explicit strict
profile is distinct from the intended non-strict public FF profile.

## Next contextual factorization experiment

The two proposed [contextual comparisons](lift_middle_contextual_factorizations.lpfragment)
reduce a larger whole Hom factorization to its remaining whole-functor
agreement:

```text
((post(b o v) o pre(a)) o S) o T = (Hom(a,b) o R) o W
    if (post(v) o S) o T = R o W;

((pre(u o a) o post(b)) o S) o T = (Hom(a,b) o R) o W
    if (pre(u) o S) o T = R o W.
```

These are sufficient proof-time factorizations, not cancellation principles
or direct mate postulates. They retain actual arrows and endpoint constraints.
Their purpose is to expose inclusion-image and strict-square computation in
the residual side condition when association and prior normalization have
hidden the smaller factorization patterns.

positives, two perturbed-arrow negatives, and two raw-naturality negatives.

actual unit, counit, and mate targets independently exercise the candidate.

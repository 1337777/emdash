## What was implemented

The [core patch](production_native_homd_projection.patch) makes the three
identity-core rungs stable owners, retaining the prior constructions as
separately readable bodies:

```text
homd_id_src_func(E,x) : T(E[x]) → S_E(x)
homd_id_src_sec(E,x,u) : Obj(S_E(x))
homd_id_tgt_func(E,x,u,y) : E[y] → P(Hom_K(x,y)).
```

S_E(x) remains the actual transformation category with Q coefficients.
There is no larger context, new section classifier, external equality input
or changed family/FF profile. The public relative root remains its actual
composite, and FF remains lax across K.

Whole comparisons relate these owners to the mate/covariance construction.
Their runtime projections retain the native heads. Simply forwarding the
source-u Hom to the old body left identity modifications with the body's
endpoints instead of the native endpoints. Native first-Hom, map and
y-component owners resolve the tested identity and nonidentity leaf actions.
Represented identity/composition instances and the native version of the
existing covariance comparison retain the original x-action observations.

The experiment also exposes two missing capped instances of existing
right-represented Q precomposition: the literal represented input and its
projected constant view. Their whole operations already reduce to Cartesian
reindexing; the capped observations had retained Q heads when the target
stayed opaque. These instances are recorded in the patch, but are not
automatically promoted independently of an actual selected consumer.

## Generic qualification achieved, with explicit diagnostic changes

The generic candidate typechecks at the original owning positions and passes
the core/Homd reference's 287 positive and 85 negative assertions. This scope
does not contain the five unresolved terminal assertions.

The [diagnostic patch](production_native_homd_projection_controls.patch)
changes six component annotations to the actual native y-functor. Its whole
agreement with the conjoint is checked at proof time, rather than asserting
that the new opaque owner unfolds. The whole-u x-naturality claim is retained:
diagnostic congruence and transitivity relate the native owners to the
existing covariance proof. No operational symbol receives those proofs.

Canonical annotations alone did not fix source-u identity action; the native
Hom owners were also needed. The generic pass is not qualification of their
entire new composition/higher-action interface or the broader libraries.
Warning bodies change from 1600 to 1636 (67 added, 31 removed), and their
remaining overlap review is explicit in the evidence. No confluence claim
or production adoption follows from the reference-suite count.

## Terminal variant and the independent failure

The [terminal patch](production_native_homd_terminal.patch) introduces a
native terminal-fibre Homd section and terminal source/target projection
rules. Its whole base transformation remains lax. The first three original
runtime assertions (8, 9 and 13) pass. Assertion 14 is rejected; assertion
15, its direct-component view, is not reached in that run.

A first-Hom check then compares the native y-functor with its retained body.
Both terms have an explicitly declared common carrier

```text
1 → Hom_{P(I)}(H,H),
```

where H is the terminal presheaf. The native whole contraction gives the
constant functor at id_H. The body retains index inclusion composed with
the ordinary Hom postcomposition action. The final runtime comparison
passes in the unchanged selected source and fails in the terminal variant.
This is a new higher-projection regression of that candidate, not a malformed
test or evidence that its three passing whole assertions suffice.

A separate closed-terminal-base represented-identity query fails in both
snapshots. It is retained as an inherited boundary, not counted as a new
regression. The earlier [identity-section review](production_terminal_identity_section_review.md)
likewise remains a distinct inherited observation.

## The relative terminal contract

For a general section s, its component is a functor F_s : 1 → E[y]. The
defined relative target contains composition with that actual F_s. Reading
its value at Terminal_obj computes the intended fixed-endpoint Hom.
Replacing the whole composite by a constant functor additionally asks for
terminal-source η. Strictness of F_s does not by itself install that runtime
normal form in this calculus.

The [constant-precomposition variant](production_native_homd_terminal_constant_precomp.patch)
does not resolve the issue: F_s need not already have a literal Const_func
head. The whole proof-time terminal comparisons remain available. The user
has been asked which contract to retain for the two general-FF assertions;
no response or permission is inferred from elapsed time, and neither
assertion has been changed.

## Disposition

Do not adopt this owner refactor or its terminal extension from the present
evidence. The experiment establishes that native generic rungs are possible,
but moving the terminal contraction to them does not automatically preserve
its higher action. The new native-u Hom's composition and further consumers
also require qualification before adoption.

Keep the selected source and all five runtime obligations unchanged. The
four passing selected receipts from `deedfa1f` rehash exactly and carry
forward. Further work must address the terminal higher-action contract or
make an explicit, authorized disposition of the old η views; more opaque
names alone are not a solution. Independent retained consumer work may
continue while the relative-contract question is pending.

Replay uses exact before/after hashes and zero-context patches, avoiding
duplicated kernels in the audit tree. Apply the core patch only to the
recorded selected source; apply the diagnostic patch to the original
join-regression suffix, then concatenate the candidate core and that suffix.
Terminal variants are sequential, unadopted extensions. The immutable
receipt inputs preserve every intermediate source and the small independent
consumers. All checker runs retained subject reduction, serial resource
guards and bounded profiles.
